## Reproduce
Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/gather_papers.py`, `scripts/gather_additional.py`, `scripts/gather_foundational.py`, and `scripts/gather_planning.py` from the workspace root. DOC (2212.10077) was fetched directly from https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10077. Original version snapshots may differ on future downloads: check the manifest hashes.

Chunk PDFs and page manifests are under `pages/`. Page images and extracted text are auxiliary reading aids under `../notes/`; originals remain authoritative.
